The canvas starts with entities Employee (key emp_id) and Pay (pay_no, hours). A pay record cannot exist without the employee it belongs to, and pay_no only identifies a pay record within one employee.

Mark Pay as a weak entity and add the identifying relationship paid_by between Employee and Pay. Each pay record belongs to exactly one employee; an employee can have many pay records, and a newly hired employee may have none yet. paid_by has no attributes.
